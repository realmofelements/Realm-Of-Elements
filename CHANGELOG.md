# Update Log

## v.1.0.1 - 4 Nov 2026
### Elements reworks
- Fire Strike: a curveable, medium-strength fire blast 
- Fire Jabs: press the move up to three times for three quick, small, curveable jabs. You have 2 s between presses to keep the combo going.
- Fire Kick: a breakdance spin that blocks projectiles and kicks anyone close, then launches a curveable fireball 
- Fire Fist: a huge rolling fire wave thrown from a punch
- Fire Leap: a fire-propelled jump. Press again in the air to fire a blast straight down 
- Fire Jet: fly with fire jets from your hands and feet. Steer in any direction, including straight up and down with your camera.
**Blue Fire**
- Blue Fire is now a toggle. Press E (or the Blue button on mobile) to go blue for 15 seconds, then it’s on a 30 second cooldown.
- While it’s on: +20% fire damage, faster fire shots, better clashes and a faster Fire Jet.
- The Blue Fire gamepass now unlocks the toggle.
- You can also unlock Blue Fire for free through a new 3 part quest. It starts on Pebble Shore. Good luck.
**Seismic**
- Seismic Sense rebuilt: stomp to send seismic rings across the ground and see distant players through walls.
- Earthquake (replaces Seismic Tremble): a heavy area stomp with spreading fault lines that knocks back and staggers.
- Earth Tomb (replaces Seismic Launch): a fault races toward your target and rock erupts to trap them.
- If you owned the old moves, you get the new ones automatically, hotbar slots included.
**Combustion**
- New move Overload Burst: hold to charge a close-range explosion from your third eye. The longer you charge, the bigger the blast, but it costs you some health and can overheat.
**Air**
- Air Jab, Air Flare, the Air Kicks shield, Cyclone, Cyclone Kick, Air Spout, Tornado Jump, Wind Slice, Air Slam and Air Wheel all have brand-new effects. 
**Water: source-based size**
- Water attacks are 1.5x bigger (hitbox and visuals) when you bend from a big source: the sea, a lake, puddles, snow, rain, or plants for Plant benders. Pouch water stays normal size. The Elite Bending Arena always uses normal size.
**Weapons on Elements**
- Hammers    Stone Rupture: rock bursts from the ground in a line and launches people
- Pike    Crashing Tide: a rolling wall of water
- North Pike    Glacier Spikes: a line of ice spikes that freezes
- Water Pike    Whirlpool Sweep
- Staff    Gale Slam: a wind ring around you
- Glider    Gale Lance: a fast, narrow blade of wind that knocks people down
- Island Fans    Fan Tempest
- Batons    Chain Lightning: jumps between nearby enemies
- Black Blade    Meteor Crescent: a fire wave
- Dual Hookblade    Inferno Spin
- A red 危 warning now appears over anyone winding up a heavy, weapon or fists.
**Air combat for weapons**
- Hold Space on the finisher for an uppercut that launches you and your target.
- M1 in the air for an air hit, then M1 again to slam them down. A finisher while falling is also a downslam.
**Weapon Vendors**
- Find one beside the Tailor in every Dynasty and next to the Elite Bending 3v3 board.
- 4 weapons in stock, restocking every 2 hours on every server.
- Buy weapons with Robux. Weapons are yours for good on every save slot.
- Anyone can buy a restock for all servers (99 R$).
- Common    49 R$    Staff, Pike, Water Pike
- Uncommon    99 R$    Island Fans
- Rare    199 R$    Batons, Hammers
- Epic    449 R$    Dual Hookblade, North Pike
- Legendary    999 R$    Black Blade
- Class weapons are still in their class trees: Island Fans (Island Warrior), Glider (Air), Staff (Monk), Batons (Chi Wielder), Black Blade (Fire Swordsman), Water Pike (Water Naval), North Pike (North Water). The Glider is Air-only and isn’t sold by vendors.
### New Systems
**Limb injuries**
- Arms, legs and torso can be bruised or broken in combat.
- A broken limb locks moves that need it, and leg injuries slow you down.
- Buy and apply bandages and splints, or visit the Doctor for full treatment.
- A small body-status tab on the left side of the screen shows your injuries. Works on mobile.
**Quest overhaul**
- Quest rewards now scale with your level, so late-game quests are worth doing.
- Daily, one-time, repeatable and multi-stage quests, with new objectives: reach, collect, defend, return and deliver.
- Five new quests: Missing Shipment, Hold the Field Camp, The Old Diver’s Strongbox, Spirit Disturbances and Wanted: The Pirate Captain.
- Two new NPC fighting styles, Duelist and Skirmisher.
- Kill rewards scale down when you’re far above an enemy’s level.
### Map revamp
- Revamped areas all over the map with new trees, cherry blossoms, lanterns, waterfalls, ponds, stairs and paths.
- Fresh colors and materials in a lot of places.
- New trees in the Earth Dynasty.
- New entrances to all the nation spawns.
### Fixes
- Performance and combat accuracy
- Server-side hitboxes: the server now checks every bending hit, so hits are fair for everyone. Your own shots may show up a tiny bit behind on your screen (about your ping).
- Leaks fixed: camera shake no longer keeps running after hits, climbing no longer stacks up lag, and the player list no longer duplicates on respawn.
- Effects that never ended: Final Slash and Eye Blast now stop properly. Bleed, burn and poison no longer pile up on targets.
- Lighter effects: Water Surf, Water Tentacles and lightning bolts are much lighter.
- Less network traffic: punches, movement effects and damage numbers only go to nearby players.
- Grass and trees: off-screen grass no longer animates, and tree leaves update less often.
- Graphics: weaker devices step down from High to Medium automatically. Big snow, mist and spirit particles only run near your camera.
- Fast Mode now stays on after you respawn.
- Combustion and Overload explosions aren’t drawn from far away, and lava is less detailed below High graphics.
- Bug fixes
**Skills**
- Fixed Fire Kick being removed from saved data and hotbars.
- Fixed Fire Jabs animations looping after the combo ended.
- Fixed Fire Fist not playing its animation.
- Fixed Overload Burst’s release animation looping forever.
- Fixed Fire Kick’s fireball diving into the ground.
- Fixed shots firing behind you when backing up while aiming.
- Fixed Cyclone still using its old visuals.
- Teleport Slash and Earth Spike can no longer be spammed.
- The Zeifung earth damage bonus and the Rozai Fire Comet bonus work again.
**Sound**
- Fire sounds no longer drag on (the Fire Jet roar was playing on other fire moves).
- Blue Fire has a new, shorter sound.
- Fire sounds now load when you join, so the first cast isn’t silent.
**Saving and security**
- Fixed bending colors sometimes resetting after a failed data load.
- Removed an exploit that could overload data saving.
- Removed hidden malicious scripts found in some map models.
**Quests and UI**
- Fixed quest camp and wreck props spawning sideways.
- Fixed corrupted shards spawning inside walls.
- Fixed the injury display overlapping other buttons.
- Fixed weapon prices not loading for some weapons.
